The Process

What Happens in a Session

Every coaching session follows the same three-phase structure so you leave with something tangible, not just notes.

01

Tool Selection

We start by identifying which AI tool fits your work style and goals. I explain the practical differences between Claude, ChatGPT, and Perplexity so you make an informed choice — not a random one.

02

Prompting Framework

I walk you through a structured prompting method that moves you from vague, ineffective inputs to detailed, context-rich instructions that produce genuinely useful results.

03

Real Work

We spend the final portion of the session working on an actual task you brought — a report, an email, a project plan. You leave having done real work with AI, not just watched a demo.
